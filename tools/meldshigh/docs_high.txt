High City Melds
Nov 24, 2026
110 new melds and 5 level 2 runes bring the High City's five runes into wordsmithing, each designed on its own. Each is checked by me against your rulebooks and the melds before them, at your word (“I need you to police yourself on this”); you see the finished melds. Made in the order you set: “Create the melds, name them, figure out what they do front, back, crafting, and on enemies, then design the rune, then the animations”.
Piercing, Bleeding, Culling and Surging each meld with the 21 runes before them: the seven floor 1–5 runes, the nine floor 6–10 runes and the city's five. Reaping melds with 16 of them, since its five with the damage runes are in the game already. The five also meld with each other, 10 more. Plundering and Greed never meld. Mirroring is left out, since it is to be the extremely rare copier of items; say if you want it melded too.
Burned into gear, a meld gives one property of each of its two runes, each a quarter weaker than alone, as now. A level 2 rune gives its rune's, half as strong again. Two properties of the same kind never pair. What each of the five gives, from its Lexicon page:
| Rune | Weapon, gloves, ring, amulet | Armour |
| Piercing | Critical Damage or Dexterity | Critical Damage or Dexterity |
| Bleeding | Physical Damage or Strength | Life Regen or Strength |
| Reaping | Life on Kill or Intelligence | Cooldown Recovery or Intelligence |
| Culling | Damage or Strength | Max Life or Strength |
| Surging | Area of Effect or Intelligence | Cooldown Recovery or Intelligence |
Each meld below has its idea in a line, its names on a move, and what it does in front, behind and on enemies, as before.
Piercing
Piercing's blow goes on through the first enemy it strikes, striking every enemy in a line behind it; behind, a lance of light strikes down its line a moment later. In each of its melds, that line does something of its own.
Impaler
Piercing and Power. Every body it passes through only drives it harder. On a move: Impaler Strike, Strike of the Impaler.
In front: more damage and a bigger hit that goes on through everything in its path, harder on each enemy it goes through than on the one before.
Behind: each hit that lands builds a short damage bonus; the lance of light spends the bonus, hitting harder for each step of it.
On enemies: hits harder and has more life; its shots fly through walls, and a shot that has gone through a wall hits harder.
Stinger

Behind: the lance of light leaves a trail of poison cloud along its whole line.
On enemies: poisons you; its shots fly through walls, and each one leaves a small cloud of poison where it lands.

Behind: the lance is ice: its whole line is left as a strip of ice that slows, and when the ice cracks a moment later it freezes whatever still stands on it.
On enemies: deals frost damage and slows you; its shots fly through walls, and a second shot while you are slowed freezes you for a moment.
