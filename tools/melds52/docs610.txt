Floor 6–10 Melds
·
The other eight floor 6–10 runes melded with the floor 1–5 runes, and each with itself: 46 new melds and 10 level 2 runes, made the way Twin's were. Melds already in the game are named, not redone.
Burned into gear, a meld gives one property of each of its two runes, each a quarter weaker than alone, as your melds do now. A level 2 rune gives its rune's, half as strong again. What each rune gives:

Off hand, helm, chest, belt, boots

Two properties of the same kind never pair (Precise's Dexterity with Swift's Dexterity, say).
Leech
Already in the game: Siphon (Poison), Cauterize (Flame).
Bloodlust
Leech and Power. Healing you can't use becomes force. On a move: Bloodlust Strike, Strike of Bloodlust.
In front: more damage and a bigger hit that heals you for every enemy it hits; while you are at full life, the healing becomes extra damage instead.
Behind: enemies it kills drop life orbs, and picking one up gives Power's damage bonus.
On enemies: heals when it hits you; hits harder and has more life. At full life, its blows hit harder instead of healing it.
Coldblood
Leech and Frost. Drinking the warmth out of what it freezes. On a move: Coldblooded Strike, Strike of Coldblood.
In front: frost damage that chills, then freezes; it heals you twice as much from chilled or frozen enemies.
Behind: leaves ice that slows; enemies that die on the ice drop life orbs more often.
On enemies: heals when it hits you; deals frost damage and slows you. It heals twice as much while you are chilled.
Conduit
Leech and Lightning. Life carried back along the bolt. On a move: Conduit Strike, Strike of the Conduit.
In front: lightning damage that arcs to nearby enemies; every enemy an arc strikes heals you too.
Behind: leaves a storm; each enemy its bolts strike heals you a little.
On enemies: heals when it hits you; deals lightning damage and shocks you. Its arcs heal the monsters they pass through.
Second Wind
Leech and Swift. Each heal hurries you on. On a move: Second Wind Strike, Strike of the Second Wind.
In front: faster to use again, and each time it heals you, its cooldown shortens a little more.
Behind: enemies it kills drop life orbs, and picking one up gives you a burst of speed.
On enemies: heals when it hits you; moves and attacks faster. Each time it heals, it speeds up for a moment.
Marrow
Leech and Heavy. Crush it, then drink. On a move: Marrow Strike, Strike of Marrow.
In front: slower, hits much harder and stuns; hitting a stunned enemy heals you three times as much.
Behind: its cracked ground drains a little life from whatever it staggers, for you.
On enemies: heals when it hits you; its blows knock you back. Knocking you back heals it a chunk.
Leech II
Leech and Leech. On a move: Leeching II Strike, Strike of Leeching II.
In front: heals you for every enemy hit, half as much again.
Behind: enemies it kills drop life orbs more often.
On enemies: heals when it hits you, half as much again.
Volatile
Already in the game: Demolition (Power), Rupture (Poison), Shatter (Frost), Powderkeg (Flame), Chain Reaction (Lightning).
Quickfuse
Volatile and Swift. No wait between the kill and the blast. On a move: Quickfuse Strike, Strike of the Quickfuse.
In front: faster to use again; enemies it kills explode at once, and each explosion that kills shortens its cooldown a little.
Behind: leaves a rune that goes off almost at once.
On enemies: moves and attacks faster; explodes when it dies. Its dying blast goes off at once, with no time to step away.
Concussion
Volatile and Heavy. A blast that knocks the sense out of what it hits. On a move: Concussive Strike, Strike of Concussion.
In front: slower, hits much harder and stuns; enemies it kills explode, and the blast stuns everything it catches.
Behind: leaves a rune whose blast cracks the ground, staggering whatever walks over it after.
On enemies: its blows knock you back; explodes when it dies. Its dying blast knocks you back and stuns you for a moment.
Volatile II
Volatile and Volatile. On a move: Volatile II Strike, Strike of Ruin II.
In front: enemies it kills explode, half as big and hard again.
Behind: leaves a rune that detonates half as big and hard again.
On enemies: explodes when it dies, half as hard again.
Precise
Already in the game: Shiver (Frost).
Duelist
Precise and Power. Deadly one on one. On a move: Duelist's Strike, Strike of the Duelist.
In front: more damage, a smaller area; an enemy with no other enemy near it always takes a critical hit.
Behind: marks an enemy; the marked one takes more damage from everything until the mark is spent.
On enemies: hits harder and has more life; its hits find the gaps in your armour. When you are alone, every hit from it is critical.
Needle
Precise and Poison. The venom goes in exactly where it hurts. On a move: Needle Strike, Strike of the Needle.
In front: a weaker hit that poisons; a critical hit puts three doses on at once.
Behind: marks an enemy: your next hit on it is a certain critical and doubles the poison already on it.
On enemies: its hits find the gaps in your armour; poisons you. Its critical hits poison you three times over.
Brand
Precise and Flame. A mark burned in. On a move: Branding Strike, Strike of the Brand.
In front: fire damage that explodes and burns; a critical hit brands the enemy, and a branded enemy burns twice as hot.
Behind: marks an enemy with a burning brand: it burns until the mark is spent, and your next hit on it is a certain critical.
On enemies: its hits find the gaps in your armour; deals fire damage and sets you burning. Its critical hits brand you: your burn runs twice as hot.
Seeker
Precise and Lightning. Lightning that picks its target. On a move: Seeking Strike, Strike of the Seeker.
In front: lightning damage whose arcs jump to the enemies with the least life, and arcs can be critical hits too.
Behind: marks an enemy; lightning from any of your moves arcs to the marked one first.
On enemies: its hits find the gaps in your armour; deals lightning damage and shocks you. While you are shocked, its hits are critical.
Quickdraw
Precise and Swift. The first shot is the one that counts. On a move: Quickdraw Strike, Strike of the Quickdraw.
In front: faster to use again; the first use after two seconds without using it is a certain critical.
Behind: marks an enemy; landing the marked critical gives you a burst of speed and shortens its cooldown.
On enemies: moves and attacks faster; its hits find the gaps in your armour. Its first blow after a pause is critical.
Pressure Point
Precise and Heavy. Strike the stunned where it's weakest. On a move: Pressure Point Strike, Strike of the Pressure Point.
In front: slower, hits much harder and stuns; every hit on a stunned enemy is a certain critical.
Behind: its cracked ground marks whatever it staggers: your next hit on it is a certain critical.
On enemies: its blows knock you back; its hits find the gaps in your armour. Its next blow after knocking you back is critical.
Precise II
Precise and Precise. On a move: Precise II Strike, Strike of the Mark II.
In front: more damage still, a smaller area still.
Behind: marks an enemy: your next two hits on it are certain criticals.
On enemies: its hits find the gaps in your armour, half as much again.
Frenzied
Already in the game: Fervor (Flame).
Berserk
Frenzied and Power. The faster you go, the harder you hit. On a move: Berserk Strike, Strike of the Berserker.
In front: more damage and a bigger hit; each use makes the next faster and harder, up to five times.
Behind: kills keep the frenzy going, and each kill adds to Power's damage bonus.
On enemies: speeds up as it is hurt; hits harder and has more life. It hits harder too as it is hurt.
Fever
Frenzied and Poison. The poison quickens with your pulse. On a move: Fevered Strike, Strike of Fever.
In front: a weaker hit that poisons; each use while frenzied adds one more dose, up to five.
Behind: kills keep the frenzy going, and a poisoned enemy dying holds it twice as long.
On enemies: speeds up as it is hurt; poisons you. Its poison works faster the more hurt it is.
Whiteout
Frenzied and Frost. A storm of blows that buries them in cold. On a move: Whiteout Strike, Strike of the Whiteout.
In front: frost damage; each use makes the next faster, and the faster you are, the harder it slows.
Behind: kills keep the frenzy going and freeze the enemies near the one that died for a moment.
On enemies: speeds up as it is hurt; deals frost damage and slows you. The more hurt it is, the more it slows you.
Overcharge
Frenzied and Lightning. The current builds with every swing. On a move: Overcharged Strike, Strike of Overcharge.
In front: lightning damage; each use makes the next faster and adds one more arc, up to five.
Behind: kills keep the frenzy going, and each kill throws a bolt at the nearest enemy.
On enemies: speeds up as it is hurt; deals lightning damage and shocks you. As it is hurt, its lightning arcs farther.
Blur
Frenzied and Swift. Too fast to see. On a move: Blurring Strike, Strike of the Blur.
In front: faster to use again; at a full frenzy of five you also move faster and dodge more.
Behind: kills keep the frenzy going and give you a burst of speed.
On enemies: speeds up as it is hurt; moves and attacks faster. Badly hurt, it becomes a blur for a few seconds, very fast.
Juggernaut
Frenzied and Heavy. Slow to start, impossible to stop. On a move: Juggernaut Strike, Strike of the Juggernaut.
In front: slower, hits much harder and stuns; each use makes the next faster, and at a full frenzy it is no slower at all.
Behind: kills keep the frenzy going; at a full frenzy its cracked ground spreads twice as wide.
On enemies: speeds up as it is hurt; its blows knock you back. The more hurt it is, the farther it knocks you.
Frenzied II
Frenzied and Frenzied. On a move: Frenzied II Strike, Strike of Frenzy II.
In front: each use makes the next faster, each step half as big again, up to five times.
Behind: kills keep the frenzy going, half as long again.
On enemies: speeds up as it is hurt, half as much again.
Guarding
Already in the game: Rime Ward (Frost).
Bulwark
Guarding and Power. Strong behind a strong shield. On a move: Bulwark Strike, Strike of the Bulwark.
In front: more damage and a bigger hit; each use gives you a brief shield, and while it holds your hits land harder.
Behind: leaves a ward circle; inside it Power's damage bonus builds twice as fast.
On enemies: carries a shield that soaks damage; hits harder and has more life. While its shield holds, it hits harder.
Hemlock
Guarding and Poison. A shield that bites back. On a move: Hemlock Strike, Strike of Hemlock.
In front: a weaker hit that poisons; each use gives you a brief shield, and whatever strikes the shield is poisoned.
Behind: leaves a ward circle that poisons enemies standing in it.
On enemies: carries a shield that soaks damage; poisons you. Hitting its shield poisons you.
Cinderward
Guarding and Flame. When the shield breaks, it burns. On a move: Cinderward Strike, Strike of the Cinderward.
In front: fire damage that explodes and burns; each use gives you a brief shield that bursts in flame round you when it breaks or fades.
Behind: leaves a ward circle ringed in fire: enemies crossing its edge are set burning.
On enemies: carries a shield that soaks damage; deals fire damage and sets you burning. When its shield breaks, it bursts in flame.
Static
Guarding and Lightning. Touch the shield and get shocked. On a move: Static Strike, Strike of Static.
In front: lightning damage that arcs; each use gives you a brief shield, and whatever strikes the shield is struck by an arc.
Behind: leaves a ward circle whose edge throws arcs at enemies inside it.
On enemies: carries a shield that soaks damage; deals lightning damage and shocks you. Hitting its shield shocks you.
Windward
Guarding and Swift. Sheltered and quick. On a move: Windward Strike, Strike of the Windward.
In front: faster to use again; each use gives you a brief shield, and while it holds you move faster.
Behind: leaves a ward circle; inside it you move faster and take less damage.
On enemies: carries a shield that soaks damage; moves and attacks faster. While its shield holds, it is faster still.
Fortress
Guarding and Heavy. Nothing moves you. On a move: Fortress Strike, Strike of the Fortress.
In front: slower, hits much harder and stuns; each use gives you a shield, and while it holds nothing knocks you back or stuns you.
Behind: leaves a ward circle; enemies stepping into it are staggered.
On enemies: carries a shield that soaks damage; its blows knock you back. While its shield holds, nothing staggers or stops it.
Guarding II
Guarding and Guarding. On a move: Guarding II Strike, Strike of Warding II.
In front: each use gives you a brief shield, half as big again.
Behind: leaves a ward circle; inside it you take even less damage.
On enemies: carries a shield half as big again.
Lasting
None in the game yet.
Momentum
Lasting and Power. Force that keeps building while you keep hitting. On a move: Momentum Strike, Strike of Momentum.
In front: more damage and a bigger hit; each hit that lands makes the next a little harder, and the build-up holds until you go three seconds without landing one.
Behind: Power's damage bonus lasts half again as long and builds two steps higher.
On enemies: hits harder and has more life; each blow it lands makes its next harder, until it stops landing them.
Canker
Lasting and Poison. A poison that won't wear off. On a move: Cankered Strike, Strike of the Canker.
In front: a weaker hit that poisons; its poison lasts twice as long and doesn't wear off while you keep hitting.
Behind: leaves a poison cloud that lasts twice as long.
On enemies: its poison lasts longer on you; poisons you. Its poison doesn't wear off while it keeps hitting you.
Permafrost
Lasting and Frost. Cold that never lets go. On a move: Permafrost Strike, Strike of Permafrost.
In front: frost damage; its chill lasts twice as long, and what it freezes stays frozen half again as long.
Behind: leaves ice that slows and lasts until the fight is over.
On enemies: its chill lasts longer on you; deals frost damage and slows you. Its slow keeps deepening while you stay within its reach.
Smoulder
Lasting and Flame. The fire outlives its fuel. On a move: Smouldering Strike, Strike of the Smoulder.
In front: fire damage that explodes; burns last twice as long, and an enemy that dies burning leaves smouldering ground where it fell.
Behind: leaves burning ground that lasts much longer.
On enemies: its burning lasts longer on you; deals fire damage and sets you burning. Where it dies, the ground smoulders.
Tempest
Lasting and Lightning. The storm stays. On a move: Tempest Strike, Strike of the Tempest.
In front: lightning damage; enemies it shocks stay shocked longer, and for three seconds arcs keep leaping between them.
Behind: leaves a storm that lasts twice as long.
On enemies: its shock lasts longer on you; deals lightning damage and shocks you. While you are shocked, small arcs keep striking you.
Tailwind
Lasting and Swift. Speed that stays with you. On a move: Tailwind Strike, Strike of the Tailwind.
In front: faster to use again; each use also gives a burst of speed that lasts while you keep using it.
Behind: each use gives you a burst of speed that lasts half again as long.
On enemies: moves and attacks faster; stays fast long after each blow.
Bedrock
Lasting and Heavy. The ground stays broken. On a move: Bedrock Strike, Strike of Bedrock.
In front: slower, hits much harder, and its stun lasts half again as long.
Behind: leaves cracked ground that lasts until the fight is over.
On enemies: its blows knock you back; you stay staggered longer after each.
Lasting II
Lasting and Lasting. On a move: Lasting II Strike, Strike of Ages II.
In front and behind: everything it makes and gives lasts twice as long.
On enemies: its poison, burning and chill last longer still on you.
Fleeting
None in the game yet.
Haymaker
Fleeting and Power. A hard blow with no warning. On a move: Haymaker Strike, Strike of the Haymaker.
In front: more damage and a bigger hit, and it lands sooner: less wind-up before the blow.
Behind: Power's damage bonus builds twice as fast and fades in half the time.
On enemies: hits harder and has more life; its blows come with less warning.
Bloat
Fleeting and Poison. The poison works fast, then bursts out. On a move: Bloating Strike, Strike of Bloat.
In front: a weaker hit that poisons; its poison works in a third less time, and when it runs out the enemy bursts in a small cloud of it.
Behind: leaves a poison cloud that fades fast and bursts on whatever is inside as it goes.
On enemies: its poison hurts you faster; poisons you. When its poison on you runs out, it bursts round you.
Cold Snap
Fleeting and Frost. A sudden, biting cold. On a move: Cold Snap Strike, Strike of the Cold Snap.
In front: frost damage; its chill is shorter but deeper, and hitting a chilled enemy snaps the chill off for extra damage.
Behind: leaves ice that melts fast, freezing what stands on it as it goes.
On enemies: its chill on you is short but deep; deals frost damage and slows you.
Flashfire
Fleeting and Flame. Burns that go up all at once. On a move: Flashfire Strike, Strike of the Flashfire.
In front: fire damage; its burns do all their harm in half the time, and a burn ending sends up a flare that hits those near.
Behind: leaves burning ground that burns hotter and dies faster.
On enemies: its burning hurts you faster; deals fire damage and sets you burning. When its burn on you ends, it flares.
Jolt
Fleeting and Lightning. All the shock in one instant. On a move: Jolting Strike, Strike of the Jolt.
In front: lightning damage; a shocked enemy takes all of the shock's harm in a single jolt the next time you hit it.
Behind: leaves a storm that lets loose all its bolts in one moment.
On enemies: deals lightning damage and shocks you; its shock hits you all at once.
Blink
Fleeting and Swift. Gone before they see you move. On a move: Blink Strike, Strike of the Blink.
In front: faster to use again, and for a moment after each use you move very fast.
Behind: each use gives a burst of speed a third as long and much faster.
On enemies: moves and attacks faster; darts at you in short, very fast bursts.
Daze
Fleeting and Heavy. A short stun that leaves them wide open. On a move: Dazing Strike, Strike of the Daze.
In front: slower, hits much harder; its stun is shorter, but every hit on a stunned enemy lands harder.
Behind: its cracked ground staggers in quick, short jolts, more often.
On enemies: its blows knock you back; while you are staggered, it hits you harder.
Fleeting II
Fleeting and Fleeting. On a move: Fleeting II Strike, Strike of Moments II.
In front and behind: everything it makes and gives is half as long, and does as much in that time.
On enemies: its poison and burning hurt you faster still.
Vast
None in the game yet.
Cataclysm
Vast and Power. The bigger the crowd, the bigger the blow. On a move: Cataclysm Strike, Strike of the Cataclysm.
In front: a much bigger area; the hit lands harder for every enemy it catches.
Behind: each hit splashes harder onto the enemies near what it hits.
On enemies: hits harder and has more life; its attacks reach much wider.
Miasma
Vast and Poison. A haze that thickens as it spreads. On a move: Miasma Strike, Strike of the Miasma.
In front: a weaker hit over a much bigger area that poisons everything it touches; the more enemies it poisons at once, the stronger each dose.
Behind: leaves a poison cloud that slowly grows.
On enemies: poisons you; its attacks reach wider and leave a haze of poison where they land.
Glacier
Vast and Frost. The cold spreads from one to the next. On a move: Glacial Strike, Strike of the Glacier.
In front: frost damage over a much bigger area; when it freezes an enemy, the ice spreads to those touching it and chills them.
Behind: leaves a wide field of ice that slowly creeps outward.
On enemies: deals frost damage and slows you; its attacks reach wider and frost the ground where they land.
Inferno
Vast and Flame. Fire that runs across the floor. On a move: Inferno Strike, Strike of the Inferno.
In front: fire damage over a much bigger area; burning enemies set the ground alight where they walk.
Behind: leaves burning ground that spreads outward for a few seconds.
On enemies: deals fire damage and sets you burning; its attacks reach wider and set the ground alight.
Stormfront
Vast and Lightning. One hit links the whole crowd. On a move: Stormfront Strike, Strike of the Stormfront.
In front: lightning damage over a much bigger area; every enemy it hits is linked, and lightning flicks between linked enemies for a few seconds.
Behind: leaves a storm that drifts wide, striking anything in a much bigger area.
On enemies: deals lightning damage and shocks you; its attacks and its lightning reach wider.
Gale
Vast and Swift. A sweep that clears the room. On a move: Gale Strike, Strike of the Gale.
In front: faster to use again, a much bigger area, and it blows the enemies it hits back to its edge.
Behind: each use gives a burst of speed to you and every ally near you.
On enemies: moves and attacks faster; its attacks reach wider and push you away.
Earthquake
Vast and Heavy. The stun rolls out like a wave. On a move: Earthquake Strike, Strike of the Earthquake.
In front: slower, hits much harder, and its stun rolls out in a wide wave from where it lands, reaching the far side a moment late.
Behind: leaves a wide field of cracked ground.
On enemies: its blows knock you back from much farther away.
Vast II
Vast and Vast. On a move: Vast II Strike, Strike of the Expanse II.
In front: an even bigger area, a little less damage.
Behind: each hit splashes harder onto the enemies near what it hits.
On enemies: its attacks reach even wider.

Swift II
