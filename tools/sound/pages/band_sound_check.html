The band

Real instruments this time: a real electric guitar, a real bass and a real drum kit, recorded one note and one hit at a time, and played here from lists of notes. None of it is in the game.

This is a sound check, not the music. There is no tune yet: the notes are only there to show the sounds. One question as you listen: do these sound like real instruments? Headphones show the most. Your phone's own speaker shows what most players would hear.

What is real, and what is code

Guitar
An Epiphone electric guitar, each note recorded straight from the guitar, with no amp
Bass
A Squier Jazz Bass, recorded the same way
Drums
A big old kit made in Poland decades ago, every hit recorded through a microphone close by and a pair overhead
From
Karoryfer Samples, who recorded all three and gave them to the public domain: free for anyone to use, in a game too
Code
The amps and their speakers, the rooms, the echo and the shimmer. And the playing: which note, when, and how hard

So how heavy the guitars are, how much echo, how big the room: all of that I can still turn.

What I checked, since I can't hear

  • Every guitar and bass note was measured and set in tune. Strings hit hard ring sharp at first. As recorded, the bass's lowest short notes were more than a semitone out, so those I left out.
  • The same recording is never played twice in a row, so nothing repeats like a machine.
  • Nothing clips, nothing clicks, and the seven clips are all about as loud as each other.
  • On a phone's speaker the bass loses most of its weight, and the kick drum keeps little but the tick of its beater. The guitars, the snare and the lead come through.
  • Three listening programs that name what they hear were asked about each clip. The heavy guitars: Guitar, Plucked string instrument, Electric guitar, Distortion. The bass: Bass guitar, Guitar. The drums: Drum, Drum kit, Bass drum, Snare drum. The soft guitars and the lead: Guitar. The band at full: Guitar, Heavy metal, Plucked string instrument, Electric guitar. Try-out 1's fight never got "heavy metal".
  • Asked outright, the most any of the three gave to "synthesizer" on any clip was 9 chances in 100 (the bass alone), and to "drum machine" 2 in 100.
  • The band holding back is the one I am least sure of. They heard music (Background music, Video game music, Soundtrack music) but did not pick out its instruments, though they named each of them rightly alone.

What nobody has checked

  • How it sounds. Nobody has heard this. You are the first.
  • Whether the heavy guitars are heavy enough for you.
  • The lead. A real player bends and slides between notes. Mine steps from one recorded note to the next, so it is the hardest of these to make real.
  • A real phone.

What this would mean for the game

Recordings are sound files, and today the game has none: every sound in it is made in code. Music played on these would put files inside the game. That is for you and the main chat to decide once you have heard them. Nothing changes until then.
