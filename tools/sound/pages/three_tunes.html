The music itself, played by the band you said yes to, with a synth for the air. Three tunes to choose between. In each clip you explore, a fight breaks out, the room is cleared, and you explore again. None of it is in the game.

How to listen. Each clip starts soft and turns loud at 0:22. Set your volume there, in the fight, then leave it alone: how much louder the fight is than the exploring is part of what you are judging.

What changed, by your answers

  • “Heavier”: four heavy guitars where there were two, everything a tone lower, more gain, and more weight low down. The bass has more grit.
  • “The lead guitar”: it never holds a long note now. In a fight three guitars pick the tune fast, one of them an octave under. While you explore one guitar plucks it, with an echo.
  • “Calmer and dreamier”: a slow beat (the kick, a stick across the rim, the hi-hat), fewer notes, more echo and more hall.
  • “The air (Recommended)”: the synth holds soft chords behind the band all the way through.
  • “More”, of try-out 1's fight: the fight is now about 10 units louder than the exploring. In try-out 1 it was about 6.

What I checked, since I can't hear

  • Every note of each tune was checked against the chord under it. None clashes.
  • The fight measures about 10 units louder than the exploring, on the loudness scale that radio and streaming use. On a phone's own speaker, which cannot play the fight's deepest sounds, it comes to between 8 and 9.
  • Nothing clips and nothing clicks.
  • Ringing chords and held notes are within 7 hundredths of a semitone of true pitch. Notes picked fast ring sharp in their first moments, as hard-struck strings do: the guitars by up to 20 hundredths, the bass's short notes by up to 34.
  • Three listening programs that name what they hear were asked about each stretch. The fights: Heavy metal, Guitar, Drum kit. The exploring: Guitar, Tender music. The synth alone: Synthesizer, Ambient music when warm, Electronic music when bright.
  • Clip 4, measured: set equally loud, the heavier guitars have about 2 units more in the low octaves. A phone's speaker cannot play those, so there the heavier half comes out about 1 unit quieter than the first. Judge clip 4 on headphones if you can.
