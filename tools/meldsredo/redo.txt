ROUND 1

Ambush
In front: More damage, a smaller area; the first hit on an enemy that hasn't been hurt yet is a certain critical and catches it off guard: it reels, stunned for a moment, and those beside it flinch back a step.
Behind: Marks an enemy for only a moment, a ring of light closing in round it; strike it before the ring closes and the certain critical does double damage, and the ring bursts outward, striking those near it.
On enemies: Its hits find the gaps in your armour. Until it first strikes you it lurks, half faded from sight, and its first blow comes in a sudden lunge from out of reach, always critical.

Berserk
In front: More damage and a bigger hit; each use makes the next faster and harder, up to five times, and at a full frenzy each blow lands in a red shockwave that throws back everything near where it hits.
Behind: Kills keep the frenzy going, and each kill adds to Power's damage bonus and lets out a roar that drives the enemies near you back.
On enemies: Speeds up as it is hurt; hits harder and has more life. Badly hurt, it swells red with rage, and its blows throw you back.

Blight
In front: The hit is not weakened, and poison bites harder the lower the enemy's life; an enemy that dies poisoned withers away into a patch of black blight that poisons whatever crosses it.
Behind: Poisoned enemies take more damage from everything, and each hit on a poisoned one throws off a puff of rot that poisons those beside it.
On enemies: While its poison is on you, every blow from it is harder, and the poison boils up round you in a black cloud that hurts you while you stand in it.

Bulwark
In front: More damage and a bigger hit; each use gives you a brief shield, and while it holds each use drives the shield's face forward in a wave of force that throws back the enemies before you.
Behind: Leaves a ward circle; inside it Power's damage bonus builds twice as fast, and raised shields ring its edge: enemies that walk into them are knocked back out.
On enemies: Carries a shield that soaks damage; hits harder and has more life. While its shield holds, it charges at you behind it and shoves you back as it reaches you.

Canker
In front: A weaker hit that poisons; its poison lasts twice as long and doesn't wear off while you keep hitting, and sores break out on what it poisons: every few seconds one bursts, splashing poison on those beside it.
Behind: Leaves a poison cloud that lasts twice as long; whatever dies in it rots where it fell into a fresh cloud of its own.
On enemies: Its poison lasts longer on you and doesn't wear off while it keeps hitting you; each blow lays a sore on you that bursts a few seconds later, poisoning you again.

Delirium
In front: Each use makes the next faster, up to five times; hitting a cursed enemy adds a step, and at a full frenzy a cursed enemy you strike goes mad, flailing at the enemies beside it.
Behind: Leaves a hex circle; while you fight inside it your frenzy does not fade, and each kill inside it lets out a shriek that curses every enemy near.
On enemies: Speeds up as it is hurt, faster while you are cursed; its blows curse you, and while you are cursed it raves, darting at you in sudden wild dashes.

Doom
In front: More damage and a bigger hit that curses; each hit on a cursed enemy deepens its curse, up to three times, and at the third, doom falls: a great dark hammer drops on it from above, striking it and those beside it hard.
Behind: Leaves a hex circle where enemies deal less damage; each hit you land on an enemy inside builds Power's damage bonus twice as fast, and an enemy that dies inside is crushed into the ground by a falling weight that staggers and curses those beside it.
On enemies: Hits harder and has more life; its blows curse you, and at its third blow a shadow of doom gathers over you and falls a moment later: step out from under it.

Duelist
In front: More damage, a smaller area; an enemy with no other enemy near it always takes a critical hit, and is challenged: crossed blades flash over it, and it is held to the spot.
Behind: Marks an enemy; the marked one takes more damage from everything until the mark is spent, and a ring of light stands round it: other enemies that step inside are thrown out.
On enemies: Hits harder and has more life; its hits find the gaps in your armour. When you are alone with it, it salutes, blades crossed over it, then lunges at you from afar, every hit critical.

Eternity
In front: Slows in time what it hits for twice as long, and the slow deepens each second it holds; as the slow runs out, time stops for it: it stands still a moment, a clock's face over it (never a boss).
Behind: Leaves a bubble that lasts twice as long; a clock's hand sweeps round inside it, and whatever the hand passes over is stopped for a moment.
On enemies: Its poison, burning and chill last longer on you; its slow on your cooldowns lasts twice as long, and its blows stop you dead for a moment, a clock's face over you.

Evil Eye
In front: More damage, a smaller area; curses what it hits, your critical hits on a cursed enemy hit harder, and a critical on a cursed one opens an evil eye over it: while the eye stares, every enemy near it is cursed.
Behind: Marks an enemy with an eye that follows it: your next hit on it is a certain critical and curses it, and as the critical lands the eye bursts, cursing everything near.
On enemies: Its hits find the gaps in your armour; its blows curse you, and it stares: while you are near it, a dark beam runs from its eye to you, cursing you.

Fervor
In front: Each kill makes you attack faster and burn hotter for a few seconds, and flares up where it fell, setting the enemies near it burning.
Behind: Each kill quickens you and your fire a little; while quickened, each use throws off sparks that set the nearest enemies burning.
On enemies: Each monster that dies near it feeds it: it flares up in fire, its fire hotter, and its next blow leaves the ground round you burning.

Fever
In front: A weaker hit that poisons; each use while frenzied adds one more dose, up to five, and at a full frenzy the poison boils over: what you hit sweats a green haze that poisons those beside it.
Behind: Kills keep the frenzy going, and a poisoned enemy dying holds it twice as long; its fever runs into you as it dies, a green pulse that lifts your frenzy a step.
On enemies: Speeds up as it is hurt; poisons you. Hurt, it sweats poison, a haze left where it stands that poisons you as you cross it.

Fury
In front: Each use makes the next faster, up to five times, and the frenzy fades one step at a time instead of all at once; at a full frenzy a red fury burns round you, striking the enemies that stand close.
Behind: Kills keep the frenzy going twice as long, and each kill leaves an ember of fury where it fell: walk over it and your frenzy climbs a step.
On enemies: Speeds up as it is hurt, and once fast it stays fast; badly hurt, it roars, red steam rising off it, and charges you.

Glide
In front: Faster to use again; frost damage that chills, each chilled enemy it hits gives you a little speed, and after each use you skate, leaving a trail of ice that chills what crosses it.
Behind: Leaves ice that slows enemies but speeds you while you stand on it; while on it you slide, and enemies you slide past are chilled and knocked aside.
On enemies: Moves and attacks faster; deals frost damage and slows you. It lays a strip of ice where it goes, which chills you as you cross it.

Grudge
In front: Curses what it hits for twice as long, and the curse deepens each second it holds; after three of your blows on a cursed enemy the grudge is sealed: a dark sigil burns on it, and each blow you land on it strikes it again a moment later.
Behind: Leaves a hex circle that lasts twice as long; an enemy that leaves it stays cursed, and a black chain follows it back to the circle, dragging at it.
On enemies: Its poison, burning and chill last longer on you; its curse on you lasts twice as long. It holds a grudge: strike it and a red mark burns over you, and its next blow on you lands twice as hard.

Haymaker
In front: More damage and a bigger hit that lands sooner, with less wind-up; what it hits is knocked flying, thrown far back into what stands behind it, which is struck too.
Behind: Power's damage bonus builds twice as fast and fades in half the time; at its full, your blow lands with a thunderous crack, a ring of dust that staggers everything near.
On enemies: Hits harder and has more life; its blows come with less warning, and every third is a haymaker that knocks you down, stunned for a moment.

Hourglass
In front: Everything it puts on enemies lasts half again as long, and every poison, burn or chill on you wears off a third sooner; an hourglass turns over each enemy it hits, and when its sand runs out all that is left of its poison and burning strikes at once.
Behind: What it leaves on the ground lasts half again as long and works a third faster; an hourglass stands in it, and as its sand runs out the ground flares up once more, striking everything inside.
On enemies: Its poison, burning and chill last longer on you and hurt you faster too; an hourglass hangs over you while they last, and when its sand runs out all that is left of them strikes you at once.

Lamprey

On enemies: Attacks twice; heals when it hits you, its second blow twice as much, and its second bite latches on: a red line drains your life into it until you get away from it.

On enemies: Heals when it hits you; its hits find the gaps in your armour, and every third blow lances you: a thin red beam runs from you to it, and you bleed.

Manacle
