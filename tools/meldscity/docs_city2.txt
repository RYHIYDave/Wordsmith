The City's Runes with the Floor 6–10 Runes
·
44 new melds bring the city's five runes together with the floor 6–10 runes (Twin, Leech, Volatile, Precise, Frenzied, Guarding, Lasting, Fleeting and Vast). Implode, Pulling and Volatile, is in the game already.
Burned into gear, a meld gives one property of each of its two runes, each a quarter weaker than alone, as now. Two properties of the same kind never pair.
Each meld below has its idea in a line, its names on a move, and what it does in front, behind and on enemies, as before.
Pulling
Backwash
Pulling and Twin. A pull, and a second before they find their feet. On a move: Backwash Strike, Strike of the Backwash.
In front: strikes twice, each time weaker; each strike drags what it hits toward where it struck, and an enemy dragged by both is staggered.
Behind: leaves a vortex that gives a second, sharp pull a moment after it forms.
On enemies: attacks twice; each blow drags you toward it.
Gullet
Pulling and Leech. Drag them close, and drink. On a move: Gullet Strike, Strike of the Gullet.
In front: heals you for every enemy hit, and again for every enemy it drags in.
Behind: leaves a vortex; enemies that die in it drop life orbs.
On enemies: heals when it hits you; its blows drag you toward it.
Harpoon
Pulling and Precise. One line, one catch, one sure blow. On a move: Harpoon Strike, Strike of the Harpoon.
In front: more damage, a smaller area; drags what it hits toward where it struck, and an enemy dragged into the middle is marked: your next hit on it is a certain critical.
Behind: marks an enemy, and leaves a vortex that drags the marked one twice as hard.
On enemies: its hits find the gaps in your armour; its blows drag you toward it.
Whirlpool
Pulling and Frenzied. The faster you go, the harder it pulls. On a move: Whirlpool Strike, Strike of the Whirlpool.
In front: each use makes the next faster, up to five times; each step of the frenzy drags from farther.
Behind: kills keep the frenzy going; its vortex pulls harder for each step of the frenzy.
On enemies: speeds up as it is hurt; its blows drag you toward it, farther the faster it is.
Haven
Pulling and Guarding. Draw them in where you are safe. On a move: Haven Strike, Strike of the Haven.
In front: each use gives you a brief shield, stronger for each enemy it drags in.
Behind: leaves a ward circle with a vortex at its heart: you take less damage inside, and enemies are drawn in.
On enemies: carries a shield that soaks damage; its blows drag you toward it.
Eddy
Pulling and Lasting. A pull that will not let go. On a move: Eddying Strike, Strike of the Eddy.
In front: drags what it hits toward where it struck, and keeps drawing it there for two seconds after.
Behind: leaves a vortex that lasts twice as long and pulls harder the longer it holds.
On enemies: its poison, burning and chill last longer on you; its blows drag you toward it, and keep pulling a moment after.
Whiplash

In front: drags what it hits toward where it struck in an instant, and those yanked in are staggered.
Behind: leaves a vortex that lasts a third as long and, as it goes, snaps everything inside to its middle.
On enemies: its poison and burning hurt you faster; its blows yank you toward it.

Pulling and Vast. A pull that takes in everything. On a move: Maelstrom Strike, Strike of the Maelstrom.
In front: a much bigger area, a little less damage; drags what it hits from twice as far.
Behind: leaves a vortex twice as wide.
On enemies: its attacks reach wider; its blows drag you toward it from farther.
