Floor 11–15 Melds
·
45 new melds and 5 level 2 runes bring the city's five runes into wordsmithing. Pulling, Splitting, Hexing, Stilling and Binding each meld with the seven floor 1–5 runes (Power, Poison, Frost, Flame, Lightning, Swift and Heavy) and with each other, and each melds with itself to level 2. The city's runes with the floor 6–10 runes are the next group.
Burned into gear, a meld gives one property of each of its two runes, each a quarter weaker than alone, as now. A level 2 rune gives its rune's, half as strong again. Two properties of the same kind never pair. What each rune gives:
Each meld below has its idea in a line, its names on a move, and what it does in front, behind and on enemies, as before.
Pulling
Maw
Pulling and Power. Drag them in, then crush them together. On a move: Maw Strike, Strike of the Maw.
In front: more damage and a bigger hit that drags the enemies it hits toward where it struck; those that meet there crash together and hurt each other.
Behind: leaves a vortex that keeps drawing enemies in; when it ends it collapses in a blast, harder for each enemy inside.
On enemies: hits harder and has more life; its blows drag you toward it, and its next blow after a drag hits harder.
Sump
Pulling and Poison. Everything drains to one place. On a move: Sump Strike, Strike of the Sump.
In front: a weaker hit that poisons and drags what it hits toward where it struck; each enemy pulled in takes one more dose for each poisoned enemy beside it, up to three.
Behind: leaves a vortex of poison that draws enemies in and poisons them while it holds them.
On enemies: poisons you; its blows drag you toward it, and each drag leaves one more dose on you.
Drift
Pulling and Frost. They slide in on the ice. On a move: Drifting Strike, Strike of the Drift.
In front: frost damage that chills, then freezes; drags what it hits toward where it struck, chilled enemies twice as far, and those that slide into each other freeze (never a boss).
Behind: leaves an icy vortex; enemies drawn across it are slowed, and those that reach its middle freeze for a moment (never a boss).
On enemies: deals frost damage and slows you; its blows drag you toward it, twice as far while you are chilled.
Firewhirl
Pulling and Flame. A whirl of fire that feeds on what it gathers. On a move: Firewhirl Strike, Strike of the Firewhirl.
In front: fire damage that explodes and sets enemies burning; the blast drags enemies at its edge into its middle, and those it pulls in burn hotter.
Behind: leaves a vortex of fire that draws enemies in and sets burning whatever it holds.
On enemies: deals fire damage and sets you burning; its blows drag you toward it, and each drag sets you burning again.
Lodestone
Pulling and Lightning. The bolt drags what it touches along. On a move: Lodestone Strike, Strike of the Lodestone.
In front: lightning damage that arcs to nearby enemies; every enemy an arc strikes is dragged toward the one it leapt from.
Behind: leaves a storm that draws enemies in; its bolts strike first the ones it has pulled closest.
On enemies: deals lightning damage and shocks you; its blows drag you toward it, farther while you are shocked.
Riptide
Pulling and Swift. A current too quick to stand against. On a move: Riptide Strike, Strike of the Riptide.
In front: faster to use again; drags what it hits toward where it struck, and each use within a second of the last pulls from farther, up to three times.
Behind: each use gives you a burst of speed; while it lasts, enemies near you are drawn along in your wake.
On enemies: moves and attacks faster; its blows drag you toward it, and it rushes in after each drag.
Gravity
Pulling and Heavy. The ground itself pulls everything in. On a move: Gravity Strike, Strike of Gravity.
In front: slower, hits much harder and stuns; drags in everything near where it struck, not only what it hits, and stuns those it pulls in for longer.
Behind: leaves a vortex over cracked ground; what it draws in is staggered again and again.
On enemies: its blows pull you in instead of knocking you back, and its next blow lands harder.
Pulling II
Pulling and Pulling. On a move: Pulling II Strike, Strike of the Vortex II.
In front: drags the enemies it hits toward where it struck, from half as far again.
Behind: leaves a vortex half as wide and strong again.
On enemies: its blows drag you toward it, half as far again.
Splitting
Splinter

Behind: where it ends, shards fly out in every direction; each shard that lands builds Power's damage bonus.
On enemies: hits harder and has more life; splits in two when it dies, and its halves keep its strength.

Behind: where it ends, shards fly out and each leaves a small cloud of poison where it lands.
On enemies: poisons you; splits in two when it dies, and each half leaves a puff of poison.

Behind: where it ends, shards of ice fly out and each leaves a patch of ice that slows.
On enemies: deals frost damage and slows you; splits in two when it dies, and its halves leave ice where they walk.

In front: lightning damage that arcs to nearby enemies; breaks into three copies that fly on, and each copy arcs on its own.
Behind: where it ends, shards fly out, and each one that strikes calls a small bolt down where it lands.

Scatter

Behind: each use gives you a burst of speed; while it lasts, every hit you land throws off a shard.
On enemies: moves and attacks faster; splits in two when it dies, and its halves are faster still.
Rubble

Behind: where it ends, chunks fly out and crack the ground where each lands, staggering enemies that cross.

Splitting and Splitting. On a move: Splitting II Strike, Strike of Shards II.

Hexing
Doom
Hexing and Power. A curse that deepens with every blow. On a move: Doom Strike, Strike of Doom.
In front: more damage and a bigger hit that curses; each hit on a cursed enemy deepens its curse, up to three times.
Behind: leaves a hex circle where enemies deal less damage; each hit you land on an enemy inside builds Power's damage bonus twice as fast.
On enemies: hits harder and has more life; its blows curse you, and it hits a cursed you harder still.
Plague

In front: a weaker hit that poisons and curses; a cursed enemy that dies passes its curse and its poison to the nearest enemy.
Behind: leaves a hex circle that poisons; enemies inside deal less damage and are poisoned while they stand in it.
On enemies: poisons you and curses you; while you are cursed its poison hurts you faster.
Hoarfrost
Hexing and Frost. A cold that settles into the bones. On a move: Hoarfrost Strike, Strike of Hoarfrost.

On enemies: deals frost damage and slows you; its blows curse you, and the curse lasts twice as long while you are chilled.
Brimstone

Behind: leaves a burning hex circle; enemies inside deal less damage and are set burning.

Jinx
Hexing and Lightning. Bad luck that jumps from one to the next. On a move: Jinxed Strike, Strike of the Jinx.
In front: lightning damage that arcs to nearby enemies; each arc carries the curse to the enemy it strikes.
Behind: leaves a storm inside a hex circle; enemies inside deal less damage, and its bolts strike cursed enemies first.
On enemies: deals lightning damage and shocks you; its blows curse you, and its arcs reach you from farther while you are cursed.
Whisper
Hexing and Swift. A curse said quickly, again and again. On a move: Whispered Strike, Strike of Whispers.
In front: faster to use again; curses what it hits, and hitting an enemy already cursed takes a little more off its wait.
Behind: each use gives you a burst of speed; while it lasts, enemies you pass close by are cursed.
On enemies: moves and attacks faster; its blows curse you, and it moves faster still while you are cursed.
Burden

In front: slower, hits much harder and stuns, and curses; the curse lasts twice as long on a stunned enemy.
Behind: leaves cracked ground inside a hex circle; enemies inside deal less damage, and those it staggers are cursed.
On enemies: its blows knock you back and curse you; you are knocked farther while you are cursed.

Hexing and Hexing. On a move: Hexing II Strike, Strike of Hexes II.
In front: its curse is half as strong again.
Behind: its hex circle is half as wide and strong again.
On enemies: its curse on you is half as strong again.
Stilling
Stasis
Stilling and Power. The blow is held, then lands again. On a move: Stasis Strike, Strike of Stasis.
In front: more damage and a bigger hit that slows what it hits in time; when the slow ends, a quarter of the damage it took lands again.
Behind: leaves a bubble where enemies and their shots crawl; while you stand in it, Power's damage bonus doesn't fade.
On enemies: hits harder and has more life; its blows slow your cooldowns, and it hits harder the slower they are.
Stagnation

In front: a weaker hit that poisons and slows what it hits in time; the poison on a slowed enemy doesn't wear off until the slow ends.
Behind: leaves a bubble that fills with poison; enemies crawl in it and are poisoned.
On enemies: poisons you; its blows slow your cooldowns, and your poison wears off more slowly.
Frostlock

In front: frost damage that chills, then freezes, and slows what it hits in time; a slowed enemy freezes sooner and stays frozen longer (never a boss).
Behind: leaves a bubble of cold where enemies and their shots crawl; those that stay in it a while freeze (never a boss).
On enemies: deals frost damage and slows you; its blows slow your cooldowns too.

On enemies: deals fire damage and sets you burning; its blows slow your cooldowns, and your burning lasts longer.

The city's runes with each other

Behind: where it ends, shards fly out, then a vortex draws them back in, hitting again on the way.
On enemies: its blows drag you toward it; splits in two when it dies, and both halves drag you.

On enemies: its blows drag you toward it and curse you.
