# Wordsmith: Privacy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Wordsmith is a game you play on your own device. It does not collect, keep or share any information about you.

- **No data collected.** The game does not gather personal information, contacts, location, photos, or anything about how you play.
- **Buying heroes.** Three heroes, the Warlock, the Tinkerer and the Brawler, can be bought in the game for $0.99 each, through Google Play. Google takes the payment, under [Google's own privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); the game never sees your card or payment details. The game only asks Google Play which heroes you own, so that they unlock on your device.
- **No internet of its own.** The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It goes online only through Google Play, to buy a hero or to check which heroes you own.
- **Your progress stays with you.** Your heroes and saved games are kept only on your own device. Uninstalling the game deletes them. Heroes you bought stay yours: Google Play unlocks them again when you reinstall.
- **No permissions asked.** The game asks you for no permissions on your phone. (It carries Google Play's billing permission, which only lets it open Google Play's purchase screen.)
- **Children.** The same holds for every player, whatever their age: nothing is collected from anyone.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before any version of the game that changes it is released.

Questions: write to the developer at the email address on the game's Google Play page.
