# The gameplay rulebook: the interview

From the main chat, which he made the game's director for gameplay on 8 Oct 2026. Nothing here
changes the game by itself. This file is the record of how the gameplay rulebook is being found:
every question put to the owner, his answers in his exact words, and how each was read. The
rulebook itself will be his doc, with a copy in the repository, once he is happy with it. It is
made the way the art rulebook was (`docs/art/interview.md` on the branch `art/rulebook`; the
rulebook is the project doc `claude/art_rulebook.md`): rounds of four questions as pop-ups with
options, our pick marked first and his own words always possible; each round read back to him in
plain words; then a first draft for his yes.

## Round 1 (asked 08:45, answered 08:49)

The questions, as put to him:

1. "Round 1 of the gameplay rulebook. Minute to minute, how should a fight feel?" Mow down hordes
   ("Big packs that melt. You feel powerful; the danger is crowds and bosses (Diablo, Path of
   Exile)."), Every fight counts ("Fewer, tougher enemies. Read their wind-ups, dodge, punish.
   Mistakes hurt (Hades, Dead Cells)."), Tense early, godlike late ("At the start of a run every
   fight counts; by the end of it you mow them down.").
2. "When a hero dies, what should happen?" Hero gone, words kept (Recommended) ("As now: the hero
   and what they carry are lost. Lexicon words and stash gear go to the next hero."), Back to
   town, lose a little ("The hero lives on. You wake in town and lose some gold, or the words in
   your attacks."), Player picks a mode ("Normal (back to town) or Hardcore (hero gone), chosen
   when you make a hero.").
3. "How long should one sitting be on a phone?" One dungeon, 10-15 min (Recommended) ("A whole
   dungeon fits a break. Town in between to wordsmith and gear up."), Quick, 5 minutes ("Short
   dungeons. In and out."), Long dives, 30+ min ("Big dungeons that take a good while each.").
4. "In the long run, what is the player chasing?" Go deeper forever ("Endless dungeons that keep
   getting harder. Your best depth is your score."), Beat the final boss ("A set number of floors
   and a last boss. Then new heroes and harder modes."), Both ("A final boss to beat; after it,
   endless depth for the brave.").

His answers:

- **Fights:** "Tense early, godlike late".
- **Death:** "Player picks a mode".
- **A sitting:** "One dungeon, 10-15 min (Recommended)".
- **The long run:** "Both".

**How they were read, and told to him at 08:51:**

1. Tense early, godlike late: a hero starts out in danger, every fight counts, wind-ups are read
   and dodged; as the hero levels, gears up and wordsmiths, fights turn into mowing down packs.
   THAT CLIMB IS THE PROGRESSION, and it should be felt.
2. A mode is picked when a hero is made: NORMAL (death sends the hero back to town, at a cost) or
   HARDCORE (death ends the hero; the Lexicon and the stash carry on). (Until now the game has
   had only the second.)
3. One dungeon fits 10 to 15 minutes on a phone, with town in between.
4. A final boss to beat; after it, endless depth for the brave.

The questions, as put to him:

4. "How many dungeons to the final boss, the first time through?" About 25 ("Around 6 hours at 15
   minutes a dungeon."), About 10 ("Around 2–3 hours. Short; you replay with new heroes."), About

His answers:

- **Death in Normal mode:** "The dungeon and some gold (Recommended)".
- **What a new hero inherits:** "Lexicon and stash" and "Unlocks".
- **Where power comes from:** "Words first (Recommended)".
- **To the final boss:** "About 50".

**How they were read, and told to him at 09:03:**

1. Dying in Normal mode: the hero wakes in town; what was found in that dungeon is lost, and a
   share of the gold; the hero's level, the gear they went in with and their words stay.
2. A new hero inherits the Lexicon and the stash (as now), and UNLOCKS: new words, classes and
   places opened by what earlier heroes did. (Not town upgrades.)
3. WORDS FIRST: wordsmithing gives the big jumps in power; levels and gear are steady steps.
4. About fifty dungeons to the final boss, around twelve hours; it fits his plan of 3 Oct, a boss
   in every dungeon and a bigger boss every few.

## Round 3 (asked 09:04, answered 09:22)

The questions, as put to him:

- **Words to an attack:** "Three each, very late".
- **Gear:** "A few good pieces (Recommended)".
- **What makes builds differ:** "Skill tree and words" (his own words).

**How they were read, and told to him at 09:24:**

1. Three of a word forge into a stronger one at the wordsmith, up to rank V (his idea of 5 Oct;

2. Three words a side, very late: two in front and two behind as now, and a THIRD SLOT EACH SIDE
