## What was looked at

All 28 words and what each does in front and behind: the 18 live in `src/game/defs.ts` WORDS (25.3.1), and the 10 waiting
on the main chat's branch `words10` as the chat board describes them (Piercing, Bleeding, Reaping, Culling, Surging,
Plundering, Mirroring, Lasting, Fleeting, Vast). By the Lexicon's five pages: Damage 5, Strike 9, Time 5, Control 4,
Spoils 5.

## What the set has plenty of

Damage, ways to shape a hit, time, control and spoils.

## What is missing

1. PROTECTION. Only Guarding shields the hero and only Leech heals him: 2 words of 28. Everything else is attack.
2. MOVEMENT. No word moves the hero: no dash, no leap to the target, no blink.
3. RISK FOR REWARD. Every word is safe. None trades his life or his defence for a much bigger hit.
4. STRIKING BACK. Nothing turns being hit into an attack of his own.
5. WHERE HE STANDS. No word cares about distance, or about whether he is moving or standing still.
6. PUSHING. Pulling draws enemies in; no word throws them back. Only the Avalanche meld shoves.
7. WORDS ABOUT WORDS. No word changes the word next to it (making it count twice, say, or swapping its front and
   behind). For a game built on wordsmithing, the gap this chat would fill first.
