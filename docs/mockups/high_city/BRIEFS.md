# The High City's characters: his briefs (the art chat)

His words to the art chat, 29 Oct, 08:31: “I have a lot of extra usage that needs to be used by tomorrow at 2:00pm so you
can go crazy with helpers to speed this level building process”. Read as (told him so): helpers working in parallel on
floors 17 to 20; each enemy and boss asked its brief first (his art rulebook, "A new character", question 4 asks how it
moves), a helper drawing each once he has answered.

Each brief below was offered to him as a pop-up, the whole brief in the option's preview, the choice being how it moves
(or, for a boss, the brief itself). His pick is quoted exactly; the rest is the brief as offered. The floors' places,
stories and bosses are his plan doc's, "Wordsmith Act 2, Part Two: The High City (Levels 16–20)".

## Floor 17, the Masque

- THE MASKED DANCERS (small): “Gliding fast, whirling (Recommended)”. Courtiers in gowns and frock coats of the seven
  rooms' colours, white porcelain masks, grey breath; knight's size, in crowds; waltz in pairs until they see you, then
  break off and whirl at you, fast; at the clock's chime they freeze and turn to look; one attack, a whirling spin; the
  mask drops, the Hush leaves them, a last bow; never clowns, undead or gory; a faint waltz round them, silent dancers.
  Being drawn on `mockup/masque-dancers`.
- THE FOOTMEN (medium): “Brisk and stiff (Recommended)”. Powdered wig, braided coat, white gloves, a silver tray; a little
  taller than the knight, fewer; a brisk, stiff, upright march; two attacks, the tray flung spinning like a disc and a lit
  candelabrum swung overhead; the tray clatters down, to their knees, the Hush leaves them; never the palace guard or comic
  butlers; the tray's clang, the candles' hiss. `mockup/masque-footmen`.
- THE SILENT ORDER, one design for floors 17, 19 and 20: “Still, then sudden (Recommended)”. Black velvet hooded cloaks,
  the blank white mask with no mouth; on 17 cloaked guests at the ball, on 19 the Order's last armed with cloaks over
  mail, on 20 only their masks floating in the fog; knight's size; utterly still, then a sudden rush close; two attacks, a
  hushing palm that darkens your words and a thrown curved knife; the mask cracks in two and the cloak falls empty; never
  gory, a clown or a ghost; no sound but cloth. `mockup/silent-order`.
- THE PRINCE (boss), asked “You said “like the prince with a rapier, dancing is going to need to be fast and constantly
  moving”. Is this his brief?”: “Yes, this brief (Recommended)”. A tall young man in a long velvet coat and a smiling gilt
  mask, with a rapier; a head taller than the knight; fast and never still, dancing the whole fight; a rapier thrust; the
  Dance, a ring of masked partners closing round you; Masks, flung spinning; Midnight, the great clock striking twelve,
  each chime a ring rolling out, faster each phase; his mask cracks and under it only grey fog, which drains away up the
  stairs; never a jester or clown, not gory; the clock, the music, his boots, no voice. `mockup/prince`.

## Floor 18, the Glass Gallery

- THE LADIES-IN-WAITING (small): “Gliding in a line, quick (Recommended)”. Silver-grey gowns, high lace collars, hair
  pinned up, a hand mirror each, grey breath; knight's size, in crowds; glide in a neat line, then dart at you; one attack,
  a flash from the hand mirror, a short beam of cold light (silver, never cyan); the mirror cracks, the Hush leaves them;
  never ghosts, witches or gory; silk rustling, a mirror's crack. `mockup/gallery-ladies`.
- THE QUEEN'S GUARD (medium): “Measured, then a sudden charge (Recommended)”. Tall halberdiers in silvered armour, violet
  cloaks, helms crested with spikes of glass, mirror-polished round shields; a head taller, fewer; slow measured steps, then
  a sudden charge; two attacks, a long halberd chop and a shield charge that flashes; the shield cracks like glass, to one
  knee, the Hush leaves them; never floor 16's palace guard, not undead; steel and the ring of glass. `mockup/queens-guard`.
- YOUR REFLECTIONS: “Glassy copy of you (Recommended)”. A copy of whichever hero you play, stepping out of a mirror, turned
  to silvered glass (grey-violet, a sheen, fine cracks, the enemy's pink eyes and rim); your class's own moves; it shatters
  into shards; never cyan, so you always know which one is you; the ring of glass. `mockup/reflections`.
- THE QUEEN (boss): “Cold and slow, then sudden (Recommended)”. Tall and cold in silver and violet, a crown of glass spikes,
  her poisoned comb; her mirror at the end of the hall talks to her the whole fight; a head and a half taller than the
  knight; a cold slow glide, then sudden, into one mirror and out of another behind you; the comb's stab; Looking Glass;
  Reflections; Shatter, every mirror bursting at once with gaps to stand in, another mirror waking each phase; her mirror
  cracks across and tells the truth; never a cartoon witch, not gory; glass, her gown, her mirror's whisper.
  `mockup/queen`.

## Floor 19, the Keep

- THE KING'S KNIGHTS (medium): “Quick for their armour” (not the one recommended, “Slow, heavy, unstoppable”), offered as
  "Same brief, but surprisingly quick for men in full plate". Full plate, surcoats of royal blue and old gold, crowned
  great helms, longsword and kite shield, alive and Hushed; a head taller, fewer; two attacks, an overhead chop that cracks
  the floor and a shield rush; they kneel, plant the sword, bow the head, then topple; never skeletons, zombies or the
  palace guard; heavy plate, the scrape of steel. `mockup/kings-knights`.
- THE CROSSBOWMEN (small): “Stand and shoot” (not the one recommended, “Keep away, quick to reposition”), offered as "Same
  brief, but they plant and hold their spot". Padded jacks, kettle helms, royal blue tabards, a heavy crossbow and a tall
  pavise planted in front; knight's size, in groups on the walls; one attack, a long aimed shot, an aim line held, then the
  bolt in a flash; the crossbow drops, they slump behind the shield; never elves, rangers or undead; the crank, the thunk,
  the bolt's hiss. `mockup/keep-crossbowmen`.
- THE KING (boss): “Slow and heavy, huge reach (Recommended)”. The old king in his armour, raised from his deathbed with
  the Hush riding on his back, its fog in his crown, his great sword dragging; twice the knight's height with the Hush on
  his back; slow and heavy, every blow reaching across the hall; the sweep; the Decree, rows of his knights marching in
  lines with gaps; the Crown, a leap and slam and a shockwave; the Curtains, faces of his deeds streaming from the
  hangings; in the last phase the Hush climbs off him and fights at his side; the clockwork songbird winds itself up and
  sings, the door to the Quiet Tower opens; never a skeleton or zombie, not gory. `mockup/king`.

## Floor 20, the Quiet Tower

- THE HUSH'S SHAPES (small): “Drifting, then darting (Recommended)”. Grey fog pulled into a hunched, faceless figure with
  long thin arms and one open, silent mouth; about the knight's size, in swarms; drift slowly, then dart in fast; one
  attack, it wraps you in its arms, a grey grip that darkens your words; it unravels into wisps and a word it had
  swallowed flies home; never gory or a cartoon ghost with a sheet; silence. `mockup/hush-shapes`.
- THE SHADOWS OF THE FOUR ROYALS (medium): “Each with one boss move (Recommended)”. Grey fog echoes of the Princess, the
  Prince, the Queen and the King, smaller, no colour, the dull pink eyes; each moving as its royal moved; two attacks, its
  royal's basic attack and one of its big moves, smaller; it blows apart like smoke, leaving that royal's playing card for
  a moment; never their full boss fights again, not gory. To be drawn once the Prince, the Queen and the King are.
- THE MOTH (boss): “Slow, heavy wingbeats” (not the one recommended, “Never lands, swooping fast”), offered as "Same brief,
  but slow, looming wingbeats". A vast grey moth, wings wider than the room is long, an eye on each wing that opens and
  follows you, its dust the Hush itself; the biggest thing in the game; a buffet of its wings; Dust; the Eyes, beams along
  the lines they stare; the Swarm; faster and dustier each phase, in the last settling on the last chapter, its wings
  beating the whole top; it dies screaming, the first sound it ever makes, and every word it swallowed flies home; never
  gory, a pretty butterfly or cute. `mockup/moth`.
