# Act 2's enemies made to differ (his ask, 30 Oct)

His words to the art chat, 30 Oct, 17:55: “I want you to take a look at all of the enemies and find the ones that are too
similar.  I feel like all the guards from 16-20 are going to be very similar and I’d like to vary them up in some way”.

Found (the lineups `guards_lineup.png` and `others_lineup.png`, told him so): the queen's guard (18) and the king's knights
(19) the same enemy in two colours (an overhead chop cracking the floor and a shield charge; the same build); the watch
(11), the palace guard (16), the queen's guard and the knights all an upright man in armour with a tall weapon; nearly
every Hushed kind dying the same way (to one knee, then down); the Silent Order's guest (17) and the Bookwarden (15) both
tall dark cloaked shapes; the ladies (18) and the dancers (17) both women in gowns gliding; four kinds moving 'still,
then a sudden burst' (the Order, the queen's guard, the ladies, the Hush's shapes).

His picks, each offered as a pop-up with the whole proposal in its preview:
- THE QUEEN'S GUARD: “Mirror duellists (Recommended)”: tall and thin, elegant; mirrored plate like liquid silver, a
  faceless polished helm, a violet cape; no shield, no halberd, a long glass blade in each hand; they don't charge, they
  step into a mirror and out of another beside you (the Queen's trick, smaller); a fast double slash, and a thrown fan of
  glass shards; they shatter like a mirror; the tallest, thinnest guard of Act 2.
- THE KING'S KNIGHTS: “Huge and slow, one each”: fewer and much bigger, one or two at a time, a head and a half taller
  than the knight, a huge two-handed sword, slow, wide sweeping blows. (This replaces his earlier “Quick for their armour”.)
- The smaller overlaps, his picks “Vary the deaths”, “Vary the 'still then sudden' movers”, “Order guest vs Bookwarden”:
  - the deaths, “Yes, these (Recommended)”: THE FOOTMEN stand rigid a moment, then topple straight over backwards like a
    plank, the tray spinning and rattling to a stop; THE DANCERS a last pirouette spinning out of control, then a
    collapse into a swirl of skirts or coat-tails, the mask rolling away; THE LADIES a swoon, a hand to the brow, a faint
    backwards to the floor, the hand mirror falling and cracking; THE CROSSBOWMEN knocked back over the shield, the last
    bolt firing into the air, sprawling backwards, the shield falling flat; all of them, the Hush leaving them in a grey
    breath, alive. (The gardeners, the palace guard and the knights keep theirs; the queen's guard shatters.)
  - the rhythms, “Yes, these (Recommended)”: THE LADIES never dart, they glide in their neat line and keep their distance,
    circling to flash at you from range, backing away if you close in; THE HUSH'S SHAPES never still, swirling round you in
    a tightening spiral, faster as they close, then wrapping you; THE SILENT ORDER keeps its 'still, then sudden'.
  - THE ORDER'S GUEST, “Pale court dress, short cape (Recommended)”: the hood down, a short black velvet shoulder cape over
    pale court dress (a silver-grey doublet and a white ruff), the blank white mask: light with a dark cape and a white
    face, where the Bookwarden is dark all over.
