# The storm clouds, made again (23 Nov 2026)

His words: “I've never liked those clouds so if you want to change them do it”. The game's thunderclouds were heaps of flat
ovals. godot/scripts/fx_cloud.gd draws one cloud: billows heaped on a flat, ragged base, a smaller tier on top, each billow's edge
billowing in and out, lit from the upper left with a silver edge and a dark belly; the lightning glows inside one billow with a bolt
crawling through it, and the underside catches it. It serves all five storm clouds: Lightning behind (fx_wakes.gd), Stormfront
(fx_m52p_vast.gd), Thunder's thunderhead, Hailstorm's icy cloud and Contagion's green storm (fx_melds3.gd _cloud).

Behind the switch fx_cloud.gd `ON`, OFF until his yes to the pictures: https://claude.ai/artifact/HYzWG5nxBe9oDXWCy92DYv
(storm_clouds.html). `--clouds2=1` films it on, `--clouds2=0` off.
