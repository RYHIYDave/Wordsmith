# The new classes: his brief (the art chat, 23 Oct 2026)

Branch `mockup/classes`, from v23 at 618ccbe. Pictures only: dev files, nothing of the game touched. All times
Pacific, checked against the clock.

## How it began

- 15:27: “I’d like mockup pictures of some new classes.  I want the brawler, the warlock, and the apothecary.  Brawler
  is going to be a str/dex hybrid.  No weapon.  Warlock is str/int, will transform into a demon so keep that in mind,
  apothecary is a dex/int.  Throws potions I guess.  Give me 10 looks for each.”
- Picks (by 18:43): “3 with a yellow beard”; “A with the spiked pauldron”; “B with D’s hair, and I don’t like the
  jeweled knee pads.  I’d like either a belt with vials, and bandolier with vials, or a side satchel”; “Yes, both
  (Recommended)”; “Vial belt + bandolier (Recommended)”.
- On the demon and the apothecary: “Can you fix the arms of the apothecary and put him on a skeleton.  And I want the
  demon form to look like a demon, and these are characters, the most important art in the game.  Let’s make sure it’s
  good”; “Just fix the sketch”; “Classic demon (Recommended)”; “Cmon man.  No that isn’t it.  Try more hulking.  It
  doesn’t have to fit on the same character wire.  Make it menacing”; “No?  Just use a wire skeleton and make it look
  like a normal human”.
- 21:07: “Have the demon effuse black smoke.  I’d like the demon form to change based on the damage word type attached
  to it.  So the black smoke can turn to flames or frost, or crackling lightning, or green stink lines or whatever”.
- “Closer, but not yet”; “Yes (Recommended)” (the looks by word); “More detail,Wings back,Let’s see him in different
  colors.  I think he’ll be better as a darker less saturated color but let’s see a few.  Especially the colors of the
  damage types, green for poison, blue for frost, etc”; “HER hair.  I liked the high ponytail.  Make her feminine”.
- By 21:55: “Yes, ash black (Recommended)”; “She’s a new character so she’ll have her own.  I’m going change her to
  Tinkerer.”

## The brief (asked as a game developer would, at his word)

Asked what next, by 22:07: “I’d like to run all the checks for characters and have you ask some questions like you are
a game developer to nail down the style and feel of each”. Read as: every check the heroes and bosses go through, run on
all of them; and a brief for each, his answers below (each offered as the label he picked).

### The brawler (by 22:07)

- The fantasy: “Bare-knuckle boxer” (jabs, hooks and uppercuts; footwork and combos).
- How he moves: “Light and bouncy” (always on his toes, darting in and out like a boxer).
- His hits: “Bone-crunching (Recommended)” (fewer, huge blows: a freeze, a shockwave and a screen kick on each).
- Standing still: “Cocky, taunting (Recommended)” (cracks his knuckles, rolls his neck, waves enemies in).
- His look, asked whether it should change to a boxer's (by 22:21): “Wrap the fists but keep the outfit.  And make sure
  the characters don’t just look like circles and squares slapped together”.
- Shown him SCULPTED (dev/sculpt.ts: one body of flesh, muscle melted into muscle, no ball inked off from the next),
  asked whether it is the way for all four: “Closer, but not yet”; what is still off: “Face and beard,Proportions”.
- Shown four builds (A a big head and full beard; B long legs, a broad V, a spade beard; C a stocky bruiser; D lean, a
  braided beard), by 23:06: “B”. Then, at 23:06: “I don’t like his outfit”. Offered four (1 an old-time prizefighter,
  2 a pit fighter, 3 a street brawler, 4 a champion), at 23:11: “1”. So: build B, bare to the waist, dark high-waisted
  breeches to the knee, a red sash knotted at his hip, low boots, the bald head, the yellow spade beard, the fists wrapped.

### The warlock (by 22:10)

- Who he is: “Pact-bound scholar (Recommended)” (he made a deal with a demon for power, and it is slowly taking him over;
  the arm is where it started).
- How he moves as a man: “Hunched and pained” (always fighting the thing inside him).
- His magic as a man: “Heavy cyan hellfire (Recommended)” (the demon arm hurls and grips cyan fire; spells that hit like
  a fist).
- The change: “Violent burst (Recommended)” (he doubles over, then the demon tears out of him in a blast of smoke and a
  shockwave).

### The demon (by 22:11)

- How he moves: “Feral and fast” (drops to all fours, bounds and leaps).
- His main attack: “Claw rakes” (wide sweeping swipes of his hooked claws).
- His wings: “Mostly for show (Recommended)” (folded as he walks, flung wide when he roars or smashes).
- Changing back: “Collapses into smoke (Recommended)” (the demon caves in on itself; the warlock drops to one knee in the
  smoke, panting).

### The Tinkerer (by 22:21)

- Her personality: “Gleeful mad bomber (Recommended)” (loves the blasts, grinning behind her goggles, a bit reckless).
- How she moves: “Quick and nimble (Recommended)” (light on her feet, rolling and slipping out of reach).
- How she fights, asked how she throws: “K this is where the tinkering is changing the character.  She’s going to have
  minions.  I think she’ll set turrets on the ground that fire something.  Not an arrow or a bolt.  That’s the tap.
  We’ll call those “Contraption”.  Tap and hold will be “Golem”.  And this is gonna be clockwork Automaton that follows
  her around and attacks.  It will just go around and I guess punch things or whatever but tap+hold will have him do a
  bigger move.”
  Read as (told him so): tap, “Contraption”: she sets a turret on the ground that fires at enemies; tap and hold,
  “Golem”: a clockwork automaton that follows her and punches; once it is out, tap and hold has it do a bigger move.
  What they do in the game is the main and planning chats'.
- What makes her a Tinkerer: “Gadgets in her kit and the helper”.
- What the Contraptions fire: “Lobbed vials (Recommended)” (little brass mortars that lob her potions in arcs, bursting
  where they land).
- The Golem's size: “Big bruiser (Recommended)” (a head taller than the knight, huge brass fists, heavy and slow).
- The Golem's bigger move: “Ground pound (Recommended)” (both fists hammered down, a shockwave and a burst of steam).

## His changes, 24 Oct (Pacific)

Shown all of them sculpted and every check run on them, asked what he made of them (by 06:57): “Change something”;
which: “The brawler,The Tinkerer and her minions,The demon”. What to change in each:

- The brawler: “His hands are tiny compared to his body.  And I still don’t like his outfit.”
- The Tinkerer and her minions: “The Golem,Her hands are tiny too.  The golem is suffering from the same issue as the
  demon was at first.  It’s too humanoid”
- The demon: “The menacing level is good, but he’s too hulking.  And the wings just have a huge triangle missing”

Read as (told him so): the brawler's fists much bigger, and four outfits not like each other nor like the ones before, to
pick from; the Tinkerer's hands bigger; the Golem freed from the human frame as the demon was (a construct, not a man in
armour), a few builds to pick from; the demon slimmed but as menacing, his wings whole.

Made (pictures to him first): THE BRAWLER'S FISTS about two thirds of his head across, the wraps thick up the wrist, a
thumb folded over (dev/sculpt_classes.ts `FIST`); FOUR NEW OUTFITS on build B (`OUTFITS_2`): 1 an arena gladiator (a great
pauldron of layered leather trimmed in brass on his near shoulder, strapped across him; a studded belt and a skirt of
leather strips over red under-trunks; bronze greaves), 2 a barbarian (a wolf-fur mantle over his shoulders and down his
back, a necklace of teeth, a kilt of dark hide, fur boots strapped to the knee, a band round his arm), 3 a wandering monk
(a sleeveless wrap top of deep indigo crossed over his chest, its lapel trimmed in undyed cloth; a long sash knotted at his
front; baggy trousers gathered at the shins in wraps; big wooden prayer beads), 4 a brigand (a long sleeveless coat of
oxblood leather to his knees, buttoned in brass, a standing collar; a black belt over it; tall boots, their tops turned
down). THE TINKERER'S HANDS in tan leather work gloves, the right a long gauntlet to her elbow (dev/sculpt_tinkerer.ts).
THE GOLEM, four builds on no hero's bones (dev/sculpt_golem.ts): A a boiler ape (a boiler body leaning over its fists,
walking on its knuckles, a turret with one eye sunk between its shoulders, two stacks), B a tread tank (tracks, a boiler
drum, a dome with one great eye, piston arms), C a furnace belly (a round stove on stumpy legs, no head, its grate a grin
with the cyan fire behind, two lamps for eyes), D a crab walker (a carapace on four jointed legs, its fists held up like
claws, a row of eyes). THE DEMON slimmed (a waist under a deep chest, thinner arms and legs, a smaller hump, his height and
reach kept) and his wings whole: the leather now runs from the last finger back along the arm to his flank and back, the
edge only gently scalloped (dev/sculpt_demon.ts).

Shown them (07:2x Pacific, the four sheets), his answers by 07:33:
- The brawler's outfit: “The brigand is close but not it.  I’ll have a barbarian and a gladiator later so we can use
  those.  Give me 10 more”.
- The Golem: “Ground pound might be one of his moves but it doesn’t need to factor into his design.  I like the furnace
  belly because it’s the most different.  The crab is cool but too messy.  The ape is good as well.  Keep trying, maybe
  give me 10 at a time”.
- The hands: “The brawlers is good but the tinkerers are weird.  She doesn’t need that vial in her hand at the moment
  since her archetype has changed”.
- The demon: “Yes, but I’d like to see more claws on the hands.  Maybe it’s just the stance or angle but I’d like to see
  them better”.
Read as (told him so): the brawler's fists kept; the barbarian and the gladiator kept for those classes later; ten more
outfits, most leaning the brigand's way. The furnace belly and the ape kept in the running, the crab out; ten new Golems,
each as unlike the others as can be, not shaped by the ground pound. The Tinkerer's hands and what she holds redone (tools,
no vial), a stance to go with it, a few versions; the vials on her belt and bandolier kept as her Contraptions' ammunition
unless he says otherwise. The demon as he is, his claws more and longer, spread and turned to be seen from in front.

## What glows

All of them are the hero's side: what glows on them, the demon, the Contraption and the Golem too, is the friend's cyan.
An honest fire (the demon's flames by the word Fire) burns orange; each damage word keeps its own colour.
