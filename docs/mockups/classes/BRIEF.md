### The warlock (by 22:10)

- Who he is: “Pact-bound scholar (Recommended)” (he made a deal with a demon for power, and it is slowly taking him over;
  the arm is where it started).
- How he moves as a man: “Hunched and pained” (always fighting the thing inside him).
- His magic as a man: “Heavy cyan hellfire (Recommended)” (the demon arm hurls and grips cyan fire; spells that hit like
  a fist).
- The change: “Violent burst (Recommended)” (he doubles over, then the demon tears out of him in a blast of smoke and a
  shockwave).

### The demon (by 22:11)

- How he moves: “Feral and fast” (drops to all fours, bounds and leaps).
- His main attack: “Claw rakes” (wide sweeping swipes of his hooked claws).
- His wings: “Mostly for show (Recommended)” (folded as he walks, flung wide when he roars or smashes).
- Changing back: “Collapses into smoke (Recommended)” (the demon caves in on itself; the warlock drops to one knee in the

### The Tinkerer (by 22:21)

- Her personality: “Gleeful mad bomber (Recommended)” (loves the blasts, grinning behind her goggles, a bit reckless).
- How she moves: “Quick and nimble (Recommended)” (light on her feet, rolling and slipping out of reach).
- How she fights, asked how she throws: “K this is where the tinkering is changing the character.  She’s going to have

  We’ll call those “Contraption”.  Tap and hold will be “Golem”.  And this is gonna be clockwork Automaton that follows
  her around and attacks.  It will just go around and I guess punch things or whatever but tap+hold will have him do a

  Read as (told him so): tap, “Contraption”: she sets a turret on the ground that fires at enemies; tap and hold,
  “Golem”: a clockwork automaton that follows her and punches; once it is out, tap and hold has it do a bigger move.
  What they do in the game is the main and planning chats'.
- What makes her a Tinkerer: “Gadgets in her kit and the helper”.

  where they land).
- The Golem's size: “Big bruiser (Recommended)” (a head taller than the knight, huge brass fists, heavy and slow).
- The Golem's bigger move: “Ground pound (Recommended)” (both fists hammered down, a shockwave and a burst of steam).
