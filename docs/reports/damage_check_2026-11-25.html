Damage check with every meld

Every hero at level 50 in the best gear the test could pick, with every meld now switched on (the High City's, the 60 redone, the city's and the reaping ones). Each build fought one elite for 10 seconds and a tough pack of six (2,000,000 life each) for up to 30 seconds. Measured 24–25 November 2026.

What it found

  • Brand ran away: a critical hit doubled a burning monster's fire again every time. Into the elite it reached 362,285,065 a second. Fixed: twice as hot once per burn; Brand now does 436,552 a second.
  • The Frost Mage's Twin Wave missed: with Twin, her two waves fan out so wide that both pass beside an enemy 4.5 tiles ahead (0 damage). Fixed behind a switch, waiting for your yes: aimed, Shiver + Twin does 60,093 a second and the pack dies in 0.7 s.
  • Lightning clears packs fastest for every hero (Forking or Wildfire: 1.8 to 7.9 s). Fire (Rekindle) is the strongest single-target pick for the Ranger, the Tinkerer and the Brawler.
  • The Warlock is the weakest with the melds of his own kind (best 195,709 a second, where the others' best run from 281,600 to 561,201).
