The Skill Trees, Revised

Your words, 17 Oct: If you only get one point every five levels, we want them to be more significant than “25% increased _______”. So every one of the fifteen talents on each tree now changes how you play: something new happens, or a move works another way. The shapes stay as they are: the warrior's two crossed swords, the ranger's arrow, the mage's three paths from her rune. Tap a talent in a picture to find its card.

  • 10 points, one every 5 levels
  • 15 talents a tree
  • a talent opens once one before it is taken
  • undone in town, 10 gold a level

New marks a talent that does something different from before; the old card is under each one. Kept ones already changed how you play, so they stay as they were. Big ones are drawn larger, as now.

On the warrior's tree, Lunge is his swipe move, the diving thrust (it was Leap; Charge when he holds a shield).
