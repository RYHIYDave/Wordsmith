## The story (owner, 3 Oct 2026)

You are a kid in after-school detention in the library. You fall asleep at the desk and dream that the librarian is a powerful witch who hexes you. You fight through dungeons and use words to push further. Each group of floors between major bosses is themed on a classic book. The plan doc's Story section lists 22 candidate books with a verdict for each. Decided so far: Frankenstein, Beowulf and Dracula are in; Alice in Wonderland is cut ("overdone"); The War of the Worlds is saved for a secret level. All candidates were checked on 3 Oct 2026 to be out of copyright in the US, UK and EU. Draw only from the books, never from film or cartoon versions. Tolkien, Narnia, Harry Potter and Peter Pan are out. The hero's look changes with the book and with gear (a Norse carl for Beowulf, a conquistador for Dracula): a picture of those two was promised and is not drawn yet.

## Design the owner has set (standing)

- Three classes, three attributes that matter to every class; build variety as deep as possible ("I want the build diversity to be at a maximum"). The weapon decides both attacks; the character has the swipe, and later a skill tree.
- Position matters for words: in front changes the hit, behind changes what is left. A word must be unmistakable on screen wherever it is placed, and combinations must be tested.
- Words are how gear gets good: early gear is mostly white so that the player crafts what their build wants.
- The Path of Exile "maps" loop: a long dungeon, back to a town hub to craft, trade and prepare the next, then in again. A mini-boss ends each dungeon.
- Controls: no ability buttons on touch; nothing fires by itself. Left thumb moves; right thumb taps, holds, swipes. Of a weapon's two attacks, the one with the shorter cooldown is on tap. (The controls experiment above changes where attacks go, not this.)
- Words are few and random; they must not pile up ("we dont want them to be held too much which would trivialize replayability").
- Art: style 6, "bold and modern" (flat colour, no outlines, glowing accents on deep blue). Animation is to be "really slick".

## Technology

Web technology with no engine and no asset files: TypeScript drawing on a low-resolution canvas, bundled into a single `Play.html`. All art is drawn by code and all sound is synthesised. Later: a desktop wrapper for Steam, and Capacitor for the App Store.

To continue the code: copy the Game folder (or unzip the latest zip) into the workspace, read `docs/DESIGN_NOTES.md`, and use the commands listed there. In short: `node tools/build.mjs`, `tsc --noEmit -p tsconfig.json`, `tsx --test tests/*.test.ts` (439 tests), `bash tools/regress.sh` (106 browser playtests; about 25 minutes, so run it in the background; its last line is the verdict), then look at the pictures.
