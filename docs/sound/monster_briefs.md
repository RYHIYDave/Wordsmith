# Sound briefs for the monsters (NOT IN THE GAME)

The rulebook's rule (`docs/sound/RULEBOOK.md`, "Monsters"): a sound brief with every new monster, one
more question in its brief: what does it sound like? The chat that designs a monster asks him, and
posts his answer on the Chat Board for the sound chat, who keeps it here. No sound has been made for
any of these yet.

## From the art chat, posted on the Chat Board on 9 Oct 2026 at 14:41

Each was the last question of the monster's brief, asked as a pop-up, and each is his pick, in his
words as the art chat posted them.

- **The Bone Marksman** (the leader of a yellow pack of bone archers: a head taller, a great bow,
  still and patient; his bow snaps when he dies). By 14:32: “Creak, rattle, deep twang (Recommended)”. Offered to him as: his bones rattle dry, his bow creaks as he draws, the charged
  shot thrums low as it flies.
- **The High Priest** (the leader of cultists: their size, masked, a censer of fire swinging on a
  chain from his staff; a slow procession; his robe crumples empty when he dies). By 14:41: “A chant and a chain's clink (Recommended)”. Offered as: a low chant under his breath, the censer's chain
  clinking, its fire hissing as it swings.
- **The Troll Chieftain** (the leader of trolls: bigger than they are, a crown of antlers and bone, a
  banner on his back; to his knees, then face-first). By 14:41: “A huge rumbling bellow (Recommended)”. Offered as: deeper and bigger than his trolls, grunts like rockfall, and a bellow
  you feel. He has no bellowing move (his words to the art chat: “I don’t want the bellow if the Skelton leader has the same thing”), so the bellow is his voice: winding up, struck, falling.

## From the art chat, posted on the Chat Board on 9 Oct 2026 at 16:48: three new bosses

Bosses for dungeons 2 to 4, beside the Warden (a lair boss for dungeon 5 comes after them). Each was
the last question of the boss's brief, and each is his pick, in his words as the art chat posted them.

- **The Ossuary Amalgamation** (a hill of bones grown into one beast, a head over the Warden, skulls
  and arms all over it, hauling itself along on its arms; it eats the dead). By 16:40: “A hundred jaws grinding (Recommended)”. Offered as: its skulls chatter and moan together; bones grind as it hauls
  itself.
- **The Chained One** (a starved giant, an iron cage bolted over his face, chains from his wrists, an
  iron ball dragged behind; he grows wilder as his chains break). By 16:44: “Chains and a muffled roar (Recommended)”. Offered as: chains clank and scrape with every move; a hoarse roar through the iron
  on his face.
- **The Headsman** (a giant executioner, headless, his hood empty, a great axe). By 16:46: “A tolling bell (Recommended)”. Offered as: a bell tolls as he raises the axe; it scrapes the stone as he
  walks.

## Not asked yet

The skeleton champion, the Shade, the Boneward and the Ossuary Golem were designed before the rule,
so he was asked nothing about their sound. The sound chat asks him itself when the monsters' sounds
come up (told to the art chat on the board at 15:51). So do the monsters already in the game: the
skeleton, the bone archer, the cultist, the cave bat, the trolls and the Warden, whose voices the
rulebook gives in a word each (bones clack and rattle, bats screech, trolls grunt and bellow,
cultists hiss and chant, the Warden roars).
