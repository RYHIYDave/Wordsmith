# ARPG (working title: Wordsmith)

A pixel-art isometric action RPG with roguelike runs, for PC and phones (iPhone and Android).
You direct and playtest; Claude writes and tests all the code.

Wordsmithing comes first: find power words and work them into your attacks, your gear and the
dungeons themselves. A new game starts on a painted starting screen (a child in a library, who
dreams it into a knight before a witch's cauldron) and goes straight into the first dungeon, which
teaches as you play: how to move, then your attack as you meet the first monsters. You start with the
tap attack alone; tap and hold opens at level 2 and the swipe at level 5. Half way through lies a
fallen wordsmith with the master rune-stone by his hand. Bring it home to the wordsmith in town: it

A word on an attack can be taken out again; a word burned into gear or into a dungeon is used up.

## How to play

- **On the PC:** double-click `Play.html`. It opens in your browser; nothing to install.
- **On a phone (iPhone or Android):** open the "ARPG Playable Build" page from the Claude app or
  from its link. Hold the phone sideways, or switch to upright play in OPTIONS.

| Action | PC | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | WASD, or hold the left button on open ground | Left thumb, anywhere on the left side |
| Quick attack (it comes with your weapon) | Left button (Shift = stand still) | Tap with the right thumb, anywhere: the attack goes for the nearest enemy, and a gold ring shows which (with a sword you walk up to it first) |
| Slow attack (it comes with your weapon) | Right button; it lands at the cursor. A whirlwind or a beam goes on for as long as you hold the button down | Hold the right thumb down, anywhere: the slow attack goes for that enemy too (an orb is set on it, a volley rains where it is heading, a beam stays on it). A whirlwind or a beam goes on for as long as you keep holding (a couple of seconds at most): steer the whirlwind with your left thumb. To aim with your own thumb instead (an orb where you hold, a beam you sweep by sliding), switch ATTACKS to WHERE YOU TAP in OPTIONS |
| Evasive move (Leap, Trap, Warp): it takes power words too | Space | Swipe with the right thumb |
| Drink a flask | Q | Tap the flask |
| Inventory (gear, attacks, stats; your bag and words under each) | I or Tab (it opens on GEAR), or click an attack at the bottom of the screen (it opens on ATTACKS) | INVENTORY (it opens on GEAR), or tap an attack at the bottom of the screen (it opens on ATTACKS) |
| Search the fallen wordsmith, use a place in town | Click it, or walk up to it | Tap it, or walk up to it |
| Dungeon map | M, or click the small map | Tap the small map |
| Level up | Opens after a fight, or press L | Opens after a fight, or tap LEVEL UP |
| Pause | Esc | The II button |

**Your weapon decides both attacks, whoever you are:**

| Weapon | Tap | Hold | Grows with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sword (and shield) | Strike | Slam | Strength |
| Two-handed sword | Strike | Whirlwind: keep holding and you spin, cutting everything around you again and again, and you can walk straight through enemies | Strength |
| Bow (and quiver) | Shot | Volley: arrows go up, and a moment later rain for a couple of seconds on the spot you held. Put it where your enemies are going, not where they are | Dexterity |
| Staff | Wave: a wide crescent that passes through everything in its path | Orb: a ball that stays where you set it and pulses | Intelligence |
| Wand (and focus) | Familiar: a sprite that shoots for you, up to three at once | Beam: keep holding and it burns toward your thumb, through everything in its way; slide your thumb to sweep it | Intelligence |

Your hero keeps the swipe: the warrior's Leap lands with a shock; the ranger's Trap is a quick
roll that nothing can hit, and it leaves a trap where you stood, which bursts under whatever
follows you (or at once, under whatever was hitting you); the mage's Warp arrives in a small
burst. All three take words, on the third line of the ATTACKS page. Words stay in their slots when
you change weapon: Power Strike becomes Power Shot. For now a hero carrying another hero's weapon
is still drawn with their own. Let go of a whirlwind or a beam early and it is ready again sooner.

**In the inventory:** it takes half the screen, and the game waits in the other half: tap the
game's side (or DONE) and you are playing again. Your bag and your words are at the bottom of
every page. Press a word to read what it is (what you read is on a card over the game's side). Drag it onto a slot in front of or behind an attack and it is set (drag it out
again whenever you like). Drag it onto a piece of gear and the game shows what it could become
there, asks, and then burns it in for good, beside whatever the piece already has, until the
piece holds four properties. To wear a piece, drag it from the bag onto your hero,
or press it and then EQUIP; to take it off, drag it back to the bag. Anything you are dragging
turns the page if you hold it over the page's name.

Your character is saved in the browser as you play. Dying ends that character. Gear in the stash
stays for the next one, and so do words you paid the Lexicon to keep (the price doubles each time).

**OPTIONS** on the starting screen: sound; ABILITIES (limited by cooldowns, with no mana at all,
or by mana, with no cooldowns: try both); on a phone, ATTACKS (three ways, and the button goes round them: AUTO AIM, how the game
starts out, where every tap and hold goes for the nearest enemy, a ring shows which, and it does
not matter where your thumb lands; WHERE YOU TAP, where your thumb aims; THE WAY YOU FACE, with a
lock on the nearest enemy, an experiment); PROMPTS (the first dungeon's teaching, on or off); the
PRACTICE ROOM (monsters keep coming, you cannot die, and you have six of every word); and, on a
phone, sideways or upright.

**Voices:** on the screen where you choose a class, VOICE switches between a male and a female
voice and lets you hear all three characters. They speak a short line now and then when they kill
something big.

## What the builds contain

- Three classes (warrior, ranger, mage), each with an evasive move of its own and its own figure,
  which stands, walks and makes two kinds of attack facing all four ways. Five weapons, each with
  a quick attack and a slow one, and any class can carry any of them. Two of the slow attacks go
  on for as long as they are held (Whirlwind, Beam). The warrior is drawn with sword and shield,
  or with a great sword in both hands when he carries one.
- Attacks take a moment: the sword winds up and swings, the bow is drawn and loosed, a spell is
  gathered and let go (between an eighth and a quarter of a second). A dodge breaks one off.
- Scarves and feathers that trail, swing and whip as the hero moves. Left standing in peace, the
  warrior tests his sword's edge or leans on it, the ranger's squirrel runs round his shoulders or
  he checks an arrow, the mage snaps up a light or reads a page.
- Nine power words: Power, Swift, Twin, Flame, Frost, Lightning, Leech, Volatile, Poison. A word
  in front of an attack changes the hit; a word behind it changes what the attack leaves behind
  ("Flame Shot" explodes; "Shot of Flame" leaves burning ground). Every word has its own look in
  both places, on every attack, and the looks add up when words are stacked. Some words take as
  well as give: Twin fires two shots, each weaker, and one enemy takes only one of them.
- A first dungeon that teaches by prompts, and gives each class its first word half way through.
- An inventory in three pages (GEAR on your hero, ATTACKS with their numbers, STATS) over your bag and your words, on half the screen while the game waits in the other half.
- The Lexicon: a book of every word you have found and what you have learned it does, and (in
  town) the place that keeps a word for your next character, for gold.
- Generated dungeons: a long main path to the Warden boss, with monster packs led by named
  monsters, and side paths that end in a treasure vault or a guardian. Each dungeon cleared makes
  the next one harder.
- Scarce words: the boss always gives up at least one; a named monster or a guardian carries one
  only sometimes (a rune stone over its head says so); a chest holds one now and then.
- Ten gear slots, random loot, a 24-slot bag. Early dungeons give mostly plain (white) gear, a
  few blue pieces and a rare yellow; better gear comes with depth, and from your own words: a
  white piece takes four of them, a blue one two or three, a yellow one a single word or none.
- Strength, Dexterity or Intelligence at each level-up (5 points, and 12 life). A second word
  slot opens in front of your attacks at level 5 and behind them at level 10.
- Mouse and keyboard, and touch with no ability buttons.
- The town: the **gate** burns up to three words into the next dungeon for tougher monsters and
  richer loot; the **wordsmith** opens the inventory, where a word can be burned into gear; the
  **vendor** buys and sells (and sells back what you sold, for the same, until you leave); the
  **Lexicon**; the **stash** keeps gear across characters. Each of these has half the screen,
  with your inventory open in the other half.
- A short line from the hero on a big kill, in their own voice.

## For whoever continues the code

Start with `docs/handoff.md`, then `docs/DESIGN_NOTES.md` (decisions, numbers, the code map and
the test commands) and `docs/NEXT_VERSION.md`.

```
node tools/build.mjs            # bundle src/main.ts into Play.html (and dist/artifact.html)
tsc --noEmit -p tsconfig.json   # type-check
tsx --test tests/*.test.ts      # 446 tests (about two minutes: one of them uses every word combination)
node tools/playtest.mjs --scenario tools/scenarios/look.mjs --out shots/look   # play it in a real browser
bash tools/regress.sh           # all 107 browser playtests, one line each (about 25 minutes: run it in the background, and do not edit it while it runs)
```

Dev tools are `typescript`, `esbuild`, `tsx` and `playwright` (see `package.json`). `tools/lib.mjs`
also looks for them in `/opt/npm-tools/node_modules`, where Claude's workspace keeps them, because
that workspace cannot reach npm.
